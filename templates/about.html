About Mukesh Ajmera

LIC & Post Office Financial Advisor based in Sarwar, Ajmer, Rajasthan, dedicated to secure family protection and government-backed savings.

Professional Background & Mission

Mukesh Ajmera provides LIC insurance and Post Office savings scheme information and advisory assistance in Sarwar, Ajmer, Rajasthan. With a career anchored in integrity, dependability, and community trust, Mukesh Ajmera assists individuals, salaried professionals, business owners, and rural families in navigating the diverse spectrum of financial security products.

The mission of our advisory is simple and transparent: to empower clients with 100% factual information, accurate premium and maturity calculations, and clear risk comparisons so they make sound, confident financial decisions for their loved ones.

Key Services & Areas of Expertise

Our advisory offers comprehensive support across life insurance planning and government-backed small savings schemes:

LIC Insurance Plans & Guidance

  • • Term Assurance policies for pure risk cover (Tech Term, Jeevan Amar)
  • • Endowment plans with bonus and guaranteed returns (Jeevan Labh, Jeevan Anand)
  • • Money back and lifetime regular income plans (Jeevan Umang)
  • • Pension, annuity and retirement income schemes
  • • Children's higher education and marriage policy structuring
  • • Section 80C premium deduction and Section 10(10D) tax-free maturity guidance

India Post Savings Schemes

  • • PPF (Public Provident Fund): 15-year triple tax-free wealth building
  • • Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY): Maximum interest for girl children
  • • Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS): Safe quarterly income for 60+
  • • National Savings Certificate (NSC): 5-year fixed return with 80C benefits
  • • Post Office MIS (Monthly Income Scheme): Guaranteed regular monthly returns
  • • Time Deposit (TD) & Recurring Deposit (RD) account assistance

Comprehensive Policy Servicing Assistance

Financial planning does not end with opening an account or purchasing a policy. Mukesh Ajmera assists clients throughout the lifetime of their investments with:

  • Prompt premium payment tracking and reminder assistance
  • Lapsed policy revival guidance and documentation support
  • Nominee registration, updating, and legal heir transition help
  • Maturity and death claim settlement paperwork coordination
