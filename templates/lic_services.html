Why LIC Life Insurance Matters for Your Family

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) is India's most trusted state-backed life insurer, offering a sovereign guarantee on sum assured and bonuses under Section 37 of the LIC Act. For residents of Sarwar, Ajmer, and across Rajasthan, having the right life insurance policy ensures that your children's dreams, daughter's wedding, family living standards, and your own peaceful retirement remain fully protected against unexpected life events.

Mukesh Ajmera offers independent advisory assistance to match your budget and goals with the right LIC policy, eliminating guesswork and providing transparent, step-by-step guidance.

Popular LIC Insurance Categories Available in Sarwar

Term Assurance Plans

Pure financial protection providing substantial life cover at minimal premiums. Ideal for income earners to protect dependent families against unfortunate demise.

Featured Plans: LIC Tech Term (854), LIC Jeevan Amar (855)

Endowment & Savings Plans

Combines guaranteed life protection with attractive investment bonuses. Provides a lump sum payout on maturity or full sum assured to nominees on demise.

Featured Plans: LIC Jeevan Labh (936), LIC New Jeevan Anand (915), LIC Bima Jyoti (860)

Whole Life & Guaranteed Income

Enjoy periodic survival benefits during the policy term plus a regular guaranteed annual payout for life up to age 100, combined with lifelong insurance cover.

Featured Plans: LIC Jeevan Utsav (871 - 10% Lifetime), LIC Jeevan Umang (945), 20-Year Money Back (920)

Child Plans

Tailored financial security for your child's higher education milestones and marriage expenses with high guaranteed additions and premium waiver benefits (PWB).

Featured Plans: LIC Amritbaal (874 - ₹80/k Additions), Child Money Back (932)

Pension & Annuity Plans

Secure a guaranteed, inflation-shielded pension for your golden retirement years. Single premium immediate and deferred annuity options with 100% return of purchase price.

Featured Plans: LIC Saral Pension (862), LIC Jeevan Shanti (858), LIC Jeevan Akshay VII (857)

Tax Exemption Privileges

Premium paid is eligible for deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1,50,000 annually. Maturity returns and death claim proceeds are completely tax-free under Section 10(10D).

Income Tax Act 1961: Sec 80C & Sec 10(10D)
